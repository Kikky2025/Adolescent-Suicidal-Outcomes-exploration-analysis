# Suicidal thoughts, plans and attempts among US high school students, 2023

## Scope and main findings

This is a descriptive analysis of the **2023 national Youth Risk Behavior Survey** student records (N=20,103). Each outcome refers to the **12 months before the survey**. Weighted prevalence was 20.4% (18.6–22.2) for seriously considering an attempt, 16.4% (14.3–18.5) for making a plan, and 9.5% (8.3–10.6) for one or more attempts. These are overlapping reports, not steps in a verified sequence.

Female students reported each outcome more often than male students. The outcomes also clustered with bullying, sexual violence, persistent sad or hopeless feelings, and frequent poor mental health. Students reporting connection at school and family support generally had lower prevalence; these cross-sectional associations cannot establish that support prevented an outcome.

- Among students reporting plans, 78.5% (75.7–81.2) also reported thoughts (n=3,189).

A reported attempt need not have a corresponding reported thought or plan: responses may reflect different recall or interpretation, and the questionnaire does not establish the order of events. Do not interpret these intersections as a clinical pathway.

## Survey and interpretation notes

- The target is students in grades 9–12 attending US public and private schools, not all adolescents. Students absent or outside school are not directly observed. The national sample uses a three-stage clustered design and weights adjusted for nonresponse and oversampling; weights were scaled to the sample size. Consequently, weighted totals should not be presented as population counts.
- Prevalence uses nonmissing Q27=1, Q28=1, and Q29=2–5 respectively, with Q29=1 as no attempt. Other or missing responses are excluded separately for each estimate. The accompanying `XXHqn` file was matched by unique `record` identifier, and QN27–QN29 were validated against the raw recodes with zero discrepancies; the derived indicators are used in the R analysis.
- Parenthetical ranges are approximate **95% design-based confidence intervals** from a Taylor linearization of weighted proportions, aggregating linearized contributions within PSU and stratum and using a t critical value with PSU-minus-stratum degrees of freedom (73). Unweighted n indicates valid records for that row and outcome. Small subgroup estimates warrant particular caution; nonresponse to some late questionnaire items is substantial.
- Associations are unadjusted, descriptive and subject to confounding, reporting differences and nonresponse. No causal or individual risk prediction follows from them. The survey asks about attempts, not deaths, and reports by students cannot be independently verified here.
- The attached questionnaire uses a different ordering and numbering in parts of its form than the coded data guide. Field definitions and variable labels here follow the **guide codebook**, including Q27–Q29 and later support items.
